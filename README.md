## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is drawn as a front page:

- **Masthead** — THE WORLD IN ONE DAY, MONDAY 23 NOVEMBER 2026.
- **Lead, Middle East** — Tankers attacked in the Strait of Hormuz: at least
  three oil tankers hit near Hormuz; Iran also struck a civilian site in
  Bahrain, injuring two; oil flows through the strait cut by about a quarter.
- **Ukraine · Libya** — Ukraine says it sank the Russian corvette Mercury,
  moored at Benghazi port, with naval drones; Libya asked the UN to
  investigate.
- **United States** — Democrats clinch 258 House seats, their largest House
  majority since 1992.
- **Tech · Business** — Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; the SEC opened
  an inquiry into Nvidia's accounting.
- **Europe · NATO** — Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles enter Polish airspace;
  NATO held emergency talks on Monday.
- **Also** — COP31 climate summit in Antalya ends with no deal; UN chief
  Guterres urges stronger rules for AI.

All stories happened or were first reported on Monday 23 November 2026.

## The program

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` unchanged. Run it locally with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
